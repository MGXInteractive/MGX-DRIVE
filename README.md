# MGX DRIVE

An endless arcade racing game by MGX INTERACTIVE.

Get behind the wheel, dodge traffic, collect coins, and survive as long as you can. The faster you go, the more chaos you face. Every second counts. Every mistake costs HP. Every coin builds your combo.

## ABOUT

MGX DRIVE is a fast-paced, top-down arcade racing game. You pick a vehicle, you hit the road, and you survive. That's it. Simple to learn, hard to master.

The game starts easy. Then it gets harder. Traffic spawns faster. The road gets more crowded. Your reflexes get tested. Can you beat your own high score?

## FEATURES

- 4 unique vehicles: Car, Truck, Motorcycle, Bus
- 4 dynamic maps: Highway, Mountains, Desert, Night City
- Day and night cycle: Day, Night, Evening, Morning
- Realistic collisions
- Coin collection with combo system
- Bomb power-up (SHIFT)
- Shield power-up (F)
- Tow truck system
- Level system with increasing speed
- English and Polish language support

## HOW TO PLAY

- ARROWS or A D to steer
- SHIFT to drop a bomb
- F to activate shield
- SPACE to speed up the tow truck
- P or ESC to pause
- R to restart
- M to go back to the menu

## REQUIREMENTS

- Windows 10 or 11
- No installation required
